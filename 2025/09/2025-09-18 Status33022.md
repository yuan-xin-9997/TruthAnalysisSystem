## 内容

"On 27 August, the U.S. Military Academy at West Point reinstalled the nearly 20ft canvas portrait of Confederate General Robert E. Lee at the West Point Library on campus. The painting hung in the library for 70 years before it was ordered to be removed, as part of the Naming Commission in 2022 to take down items that commemorate or memorialize the Confederacy."
